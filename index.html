About Me

Building Toward Web Administration

My path into web administration started with front-end development, where I learned how to build responsive layouts, write clean HTML and CSS, and pay close attention to detail across browsers and devices. That foundation grew to include email development with MJML, along with experience in React and modern web technologies. Over time, my interests shifted toward the operational side of the web: hosting, deployment, maintenance, content management, and keeping sites secure and reliable. I am especially focused on WordPress administration, Linux environments, DNS, SSL, backups, and the day-to-day work of website operations. My development background supports that work by giving me a strong understanding of how websites are built, which makes troubleshooting and maintenance more effective. When I am not working, I enjoy spending time with my family, sketching, boxing, reading, and learning new technologies that help me grow as a web administrator.
